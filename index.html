You Made It to Day 100!

You made it!

I hope somewhere along these 100 days, you discovered something I had to discover for myself: maybe you really do have time.

For years, I told myself I didn't have enough time to read and study the Bible. There was always something else to do, something that needed my attention, or some reason why I would start tomorrow.

But when I started cutting out the things that were constantly taking my time—especially TV and mindless phone scrolling—I realized something.

I had more time than I thought I did.

And for the first time in my life, I actually read and studied through the entire Bible in a year.

Not because I suddenly had an extra three hours every morning.

I just started making God's Word a priority.

Maybe these five-minute devotionals helped you do the same.

Maybe five minutes became ten. Maybe ten became twenty. Maybe you simply learned to put down your phone for a few minutes and open your Bible instead.

Whatever it looks like for you, I hope you keep going.

These devotionals were adapted from my writing ministry, Holy Parables, where I write relatable, sometimes humorous, Scripture-centered devotionals and stories to encourage us to take our faith seriously without taking ourselves too seriously. 😂

If you enjoyed these, you can continue reading a new Holy Parables devotional every Wednesday over on Patreon.

And if you would like me to make another I Don't Have Time devotional, let me know over at Holy Parables! Your feedback will help me know if there's a need for another 100 days.

And finally...

If you noticed a Scripture repeated somewhere along the way, I'm sorry. 😂

Keeping track of 100 different verses is harder than it sounds!

But honestly?

Maybe that's not the worst thing.

There are some Scriptures we probably need to read more than once anyway.

Some truths are worth repeating.

Thank you for spending these 100 days with me.

Now go open your Bible.

You might have more time than you think. ❤️

— Ebony
